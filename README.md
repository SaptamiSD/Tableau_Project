**📊 Vastra Store Sales Data Analysis (2025)**


**🧠 Business Problem**

Vastra Store required a data-driven annual sales analysis for 2025 to understand customer behavior, identify top-performing regions and channels, and improve strategic decision-making for future growth.

**🎯 Project Objective**

Analyze 2025 sales performance using Excel

Identify key customer segments and sales drivers

Build an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders

Provide actionable insights to improve revenue and operations

**🧰 Tools & Skills Used**

Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers)

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Dashboard Design & Storytelling

Business Insight & Reporting

**📂 Dataset**

Public retail sales dataset sourced from Kaggle / Google Datasets

Modified and cleaned for analytical purposes

Format: Excel (.xlsx)

⚠️ Dataset used for educational and portfolio demonstration only.

**📊 Key Metrics & Analysis (KPIs)**

Sales vs Orders trend analysis

Monthly sales performance (peak month identification)

Gender-based purchase distribution

Order status analysis (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, etc.)

Top 10 states by revenue contribution

Age group vs gender purchasing behavior

Channel-wise sales contribution

Category-wise sales performance

Order fulfillment rate (% Delivered)

**⚙️ Data Analysis Workflow**

Imported raw sales data into Excel

Performed data cleaning (handled missing values, duplicates, and formatting issues)

Created calculated fields and KPIs

Built Pivot Tables to answer business questions

Designed an interactive dashboard using slicers and charts

Interpreted trends and generated business recommendations

Customers aged 30–49 years form the most valuable segment

💡 Business Recommendations
